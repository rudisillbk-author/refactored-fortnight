About Whitley

Fueled by coffee, buried in books, always mid-draft

Whitley Green writes ménage romance on the theory that life's too short for anything less crowded. She's fueled by coffee, rarely without a book going, and firmly believes a good happily-ever-after is worth staying up past a reasonable bedtime for.

Her Sizzle TV series follows a loosely connected group of friends, coworkers, and exes through workplace tension, reality-TV chaos, and every kind of slow burn, always landing on a guaranteed happy ending.
